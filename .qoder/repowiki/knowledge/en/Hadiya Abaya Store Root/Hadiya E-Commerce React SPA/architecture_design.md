The app entry (`main.jsx`) mounts `<App>` under `React.StrictMode`; `App.jsx` nests four context providers — `ToastProvider`, `ProductProvider`, `WishlistProvider`, `CartProvider` — around a `BrowserRouter` so every page can consume cart/wishlist/product/toast state. A single `MainLayout` component owns the shared chrome: it renders `Header`, `Footer`, `MobileBottomNav`, plus overlay drawers (`CartDrawer`, `WishlistDrawer`, `SearchModal`, `MobileMenuDrawer`) and conditionally hides chrome on `/admin`. Pages are declared as routes inside `MainLayout`'s `<Routes>` block (`/`, `/shop`, `/product/:id`, `/cart`, `/checkout`, `/order-success`, `/admin`). Cross-cutting concerns (toasts, scroll-to-top, search/mobile menu open state) live in `MainLayout` or contexts; product data is seeded from `data/products.js` and consumed via `ProductContext`; media uploads go through `utils/cloudinary.js`. In production a Service Worker is registered for offline caching.
